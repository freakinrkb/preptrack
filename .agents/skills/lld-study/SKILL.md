# PrepTrack LLD learning

Treat an LLD item as a design exercise, not merely a completion checkbox. A useful packet lets a learner capture requirements, entities/value objects, relationships or class-diagram reference, pattern choices with rationale, edge cases/concurrency, trade-offs, and a retrospective.

- Keep source case studies link-first with attribution; the learner owns their personal packet.
- Allow drafts and partial progress. Completion is a learner decision followed by an attempt/confidence record and review date.
- Use structured fields for retrieval and free-form notes for nuance; do not require a built-in diagram editor in MVP.
- Do not assert a particular pattern is mandatory for every case; record the learner's rationale and alternatives.

For the source-backed learning structure and examples, read `references/learning-structure.md`.
